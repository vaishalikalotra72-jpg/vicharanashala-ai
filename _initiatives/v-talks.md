<p class="idt-lead">At the heart of our lab is the spirit of Vicharana — the act of deep reflection and inquiry. V-Talks (Vicharanashala Talks) are the living embodiment of this spirit. We believe that insightful conversations shouldn't be confined to the walls of academia.</p>

## A crossroads for diverse perspectives

V-Talks bring together people from different walks of life. We invite professionals, creators, and thinkers to discuss themes that resonate far beyond the classroom.

## From the series

<div class="idt-sessions">
  <div class="idt-session">
    <div class="idt-session-title">The Entrepreneurial Mindset</div>
    <div class="idt-session-by">Averil Spencer — Co-founder &amp; CEO, Launch Girls · MIT &amp; Harvard Alumna</div>
    <p class="idt-session-desc">What does it take to build something from nothing — and what does that have to do with how students should be thinking about their education? Averil Spencer, co-founder and CEO of Launch Girls and an alumna of both MIT and Harvard, brought the lived reality of entrepreneurship into the room. Not as a success story to be admired from a distance, but as a mindset — a way of approaching problems, uncertainty, and growth that students can begin practising long before they ever start a company.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="idt-session">
    <div class="idt-session-title">How to Actually Learn</div>
    <div class="idt-session-by">Dr. Shyam Raghunath Joshi</div>
    <p class="idt-session-desc">Everyone is told to study harder. Far fewer are ever taught how to actually learn. Dr. Shyam Raghunath Joshi turned the spotlight on something students interact with every day but rarely examine: the mechanics of learning itself. From retention to reflection, the session unpacked practical techniques that help learners become more deliberate, more effective, and ultimately more independent — the kind of session that students leave wanting to rethink how they have been approaching their own education entirely.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="idt-session">
    <div class="idt-session-title">The Perfect Time Never Comes</div>
    <div class="idt-session-by">Devyani Wankhede — IITM BS Student · Author of The Art of Doing It Tomorrow</div>
    <p class="idt-session-desc">Devyani Wankhede, an IITM BS student and author of The Art of Doing It Tomorrow, addressed a habit almost every student knows well: waiting for the "right moment" to begin. She shared why procrastination happens and how small, consistent actions matter more than perfect timing.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="idt-session">
    <div class="idt-session-title">Mindfulness for Productivity</div>
    <div class="idt-session-by">Dhivya Kannan — Mindfulness Life Coach · Founder, Ni Nurturing Institute</div>
    <p class="idt-session-desc">Dhivya Kannan, mindfulness life coach and founder of Ni Nurturing Institute, explored how a calm and focused mind leads to better work. The session introduced simple mindfulness practices that help reduce stress, improve concentration, and make everyday tasks feel more manageable.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="idt-session">
    <div class="idt-session-title">Your Network is Your Net Worth</div>
    <div class="idt-session-by">Dr. Yayati Gupta — Assistant Professor, Mahindra University · PhD (CSE), IIT Ropar Alumna</div>
    <p class="idt-session-desc">Dr. Yayati Gupta, Assistant Professor at Mahindra University and a PhD (CSE) alumna of IIT Ropar, spoke about the value of building meaningful professional relationships. She shared how students can start networking early through peers, mentors, and communities, and how these connections shape future opportunities.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="idt-session">
    <div class="idt-session-title">Ask Me Anything</div>
    <div class="idt-session-by">Dr. M.S. Lakshmi Priya, IAS — Secretary, Coir Board, Ministry of MSME, Government of India</div>
    <p class="idt-session-desc">In this open Q&amp;A session, Dr. M.S. Lakshmi Priya, IAS, Secretary of the Coir Board under the Ministry of MSME, answered participants' questions directly. The conversation covered her journey in public service, career choices, and practical advice for students planning their own paths.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="idt-session">
    <div class="idt-session-title">Hapttitude Neuromagic</div>
    <div class="idt-session-by">Prof. Pretty Bhalla — Professor at LPU · Hapttitude Coach &amp; Culture Architect</div>
    <p class="idt-session-desc">Prof. Pretty Bhalla, Professor at LPU, Hapttitude Coach, and Culture Architect, introduced the idea of Hapttitude, which brings together happiness and attitude. The session looked at how our thinking patterns shape our emotions and behaviour, and shared simple ways to build a more positive and resilient mindset.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="idt-session">
    <div class="idt-session-title">The Art of Juggling: Balancing Career, Passion &amp; Side Hustles</div>
    <div class="idt-session-by">Lakshay Kumar — Final-Year B.Tech (CSE), IIT Ropar</div>
    <p class="idt-session-desc">Lakshay Kumar, a final-year B.Tech (CSE) student at IIT Ropar, spoke from a student's point of view about managing academics, personal interests, and side projects together. He shared practical tips on prioritising, managing time, and avoiding burnout while still exploring what excites you.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="idt-session">
    <div class="idt-session-title">Destination vs. Journey: Learning in the Age of AI</div>
    <div class="idt-session-by">Dr. Amit Kumar Marwah — Professor, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Acropolis Institute of Technology and Research, Indore (MP)</div>
    <p class="idt-session-desc">Dr. Amit Kumar Marwah, Professor of Mechanical Engineering at Acropolis Institute of Technology and Research, Indore, reflected on what learning means now that AI can quickly produce answers. He encouraged students to value the process of understanding, not just the final result, and to use AI as a support rather than a shortcut.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="idt-session">
    <div class="idt-session-title">Lessons from Playing Chess for the Teacher-Student Relationship</div>
    <div class="idt-session-by">Jyotsna Lakshminarayanan — Woman FIDE Master and Chess Coach</div>
    <p class="idt-session-desc">Jyotsna Lakshminarayanan, Woman FIDE Master and chess coach, drew parallels between the chessboard and the classroom. She shared how patience, strategy, learning from mistakes, and mutual trust help build a strong relationship between teachers and students.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="idt-session">
    <div class="idt-session-title">Understanding Emotional Intelligence</div>
    <div class="idt-session-by">Smt. Amrita Murthy — Principal, Wisdom School, Karnataka</div>
    <p class="idt-session-desc">Smt. Amrita Murthy, Principal of Wisdom School, Karnataka, explained what emotional intelligence is and why it matters as much as academic ability. The session covered recognising and managing your own emotions, understanding others, and using these skills to build better relationships at school, at work, and in life.</p>
  </div>
</div>
